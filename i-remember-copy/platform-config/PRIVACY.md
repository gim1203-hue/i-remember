# Privacy Policy — I Remember

Both the App Store and Google Play require a working privacy policy URL
before they'll accept an app that requests camera and microphone access.
This is a starting draft — read it over, adjust anything that doesn't
match how the app actually behaves once you've built it out, and host it
somewhere public (a GitHub Pages page, a Vercel deploy, a plain webpage)
before submitting.

---

**What I Remember stores:** photos, video and voice recordings, mood
selections, and text notes that you choose to add to a specific day.

**Where it's stored:** on your device, in the app's local storage. Nothing
is uploaded to a server or shared with the developer or any third party.

**Camera and microphone access:** used only when you actively choose to
take a photo, capture a moment, or start a recording. The app does not
record in the background or without your action.

**Data deletion:** removing a photo, video, or voice note from within the
app deletes it from local storage. Uninstalling the app removes all of
its data from the device.

**Third parties:** this app does not include analytics, ads, or
third-party trackers.
